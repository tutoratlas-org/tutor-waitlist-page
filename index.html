FAQ

Straight answers before you join

Is Tutor Atlas another tutoring marketplace?

No. Marketplaces list tutors for parents to browse and take a cut of the relationship. Tutor Atlas does none of that. It's a personal AI agent for tutors who already have their own students. Your students stay yours.

What does the AI agent actually do?

Think of it as a PA for the boring half of tutoring. It logs your lessons, keeps track of schedules and who owes what, and writes your month-end bills ready to paste into WhatsApp. No more rebuilding spreadsheets on a Sunday night. Later, the same assistant can watch Telegram channels for new assignments and help you apply, but lesson admin comes first.

Does the AI agent handle tuition assignments?

Yes, that's the plan, on the same assistant, not a separate product. It watches public agency Telegram channels the moment new assignments post, preps your application in each agency's format, and remembers what you've tried. It never applies without your yes. Lesson admin comes first though, and assignment features switch on after that's running.

Will Tutor Atlas poach my students or share my book?

No. Your student book stays yours. Privacy and trust come first. We are not here to advertise your relationships.

Do parents need an account?

Not in the first release. You work with the agent; parents receive what you already send today. For example, paste-ready bill text you copy into WhatsApp. The agent never messages parents for you.

Who is Tutor Atlas for?

Independent home tutors in Singapore who already have their own students and want their evenings back. If you're looking for a marketplace to find students for you, this isn't it.

Is pricing listed here?

No. Public pricing is not on this page. We will share commercial details when early access is relevant.

When will it launch?

We're building with our technical partner now and collecting interested tutors first. Waitlist members hear before anyone else.
