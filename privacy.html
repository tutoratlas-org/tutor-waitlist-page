1. Who we are

Tutor Atlas is building an assistant for independent tutors in Singapore. For this waitlist, what is collected and why remains at the sole discretion of Tutor Atlas.

Questions, corrections, and deletion requests go to hello@tutoratlas.sg. A person monitors that mailbox; there is no self-service privacy dashboard for the waitlist.

2. What the waitlist collects

The waitlist form asks for only two pieces of information:

  • your name; and
  • your Telegram handle.

We use the waitlist details to contact you about early access to Tutor Atlas. Your data is only collected and processed for the express purposes mentioned on this website, and not for any other purposes other than those expressed.

3. Who processes the waitlist

The waitlist is handled by Basin. Basin is the form processor. Basin is operated from Canada, uses managed PostgreSQL in Canada, and uses AWS in the United States. That means your waitlist details may be processed outside Singapore, including in Canada and the United States.

Basin's data processing agreement is part of Basin's terms. The public form endpoint is https://usebasin.com/f/0184c01ee34e. We rely on that agreement for Basin's handling of the waitlist details and for overseas processing by Basin and its subprocessors.

Basin names subprocessors including AWS, Postmark, Stripe, DigitalOcean, Cloudflare, Cloud 66, CleanTalk, Google reCAPTCHA, hCaptcha, Cloudflare Turnstile, and the OpenAI API. Not every subprocessor receives every waitlist submission.

People who handle personal data for Tutor Atlas must keep it confidential and use it only for the purposes in this notice. Where needed, we may also disclose personal data to service vendors (including overseas vendors for storage, mail, research, or disaster recovery), parties involved in a dispute, parties who help investigate or administer a matter you raise, and industry or government bodies when the law requires it.

If we process personal data of people in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, we will put contractual protections in place before that processing. We do not claim that standard contractual clauses are already signed.

4. How long we keep it

We keep a waitlist submission for 365 days, unless you ask us to delete it earlier. Our operational requirement is for the form processor to apply the same 365-day period.

When you use the assistant, product data is kept while you use the service and is deleted on request, except where the law requires a longer hold. If the product retention period will differ from that description, the exact period will be stated on this page.

5. How to ask for deletion

Email hello@tutoratlas.sg and tell us the name or Telegram handle you used for the waitlist. We will look up the submission manually and confirm the request through the submitted Telegram account before deleting the matching waitlist entry from the records we control.

Because a Telegram handle is a contact route, not a stable account key, we will not delete a waitlist entry based only on an emailed name or public handle.

6. When you use the assistant

The waitlist and the assistant are separate. The waitlist fields collected today are name and Telegram handle only, for early-access contact as set out above.

When you use the assistant, these are express purposes mentioned on this website: to evaluate, process, and administer your use of Tutor Atlas; to run a personalised AI agent on lesson logs, schedule information, payment state, and related student or parent details that you enter; to help you prepare paste-ready WhatsApp bills for you to send; later, to help you watch public agency Telegram channels for assignments without sending an application unless you confirm; to communicate with you about the service; to prevent fraud or misuse where needed; to carry out limited internal quality and operational checks; and to comply with law and respond to regulators when required.

Tutor Atlas is for tutors, not for children to sign up. Students are often minors. When you use the assistant, you may only enter a student's or parent's details if you already have a proper basis to hold them for teaching and billing. A message you yourself send (for example a WhatsApp bill, or an agency application you confirm) is your disclosure.

We do not sell personal data. We do not share personal data for advertising. We do not combine a tutor's book with other customers' data to advertise. We do not use waitlist data for third-party marketing. Early-access contact is the only waitlist message. You can ask us to stop contacting you by emailing hello@tutoratlas.sg.

Before the assistant starts collecting lesson or student data, any AI or hosting providers that process that data for us will be named on this page. We do not claim those processor contracts exist today.

7. The AI agent

Tutor Atlas involves an AI agent. Its job is to help an independent home tutor in Singapore with lesson logs, schedule, payment state, paste-ready WhatsApp bills the tutor sends, and later assignment watching on public agency Telegram channels.

The agent is bounded. It drafts bills for you to copy into WhatsApp. It does not message parents. It does not send an assignment application unless you confirm. It does not list your book on a marketplace. Risk is limited up front by narrow tools and by confirmation before outbound messages.

Humans stay accountable. Tutor Atlas is accountable for how the agent is designed and run. You are accountable for what you confirm and send, and for having the right to enter student or parent details. Recourse is hello@tutoratlas.sg, not "the algorithm".

Singapore's Model AI Governance Framework for Agentic AI is non-binding guidance. It does not replace the PDPA. The PDPA still binds how we handle personal data.

8. Access, correction, and other rights

You may ask to access or correct your personal data, or withdraw consent, by emailing hello@tutoratlas.sg. We may charge a reasonable fee for access or correction where the PDPA allows it, and we will tell you the fee in advance if one applies. Deletion of waitlist entries follows section 5.

If European data-protection law protects you, you may also have rights to erasure, restriction, and objection, and the right to complain to your supervisory authority.

9. Security and breaches

We take security measures appropriate to a waitlist today. When the assistant collects lesson or student data, we will apply security appropriate to that processing. We do not claim specific certifications or contractual breach timelines in this notice.

If a data breach occurs and the PDPA requires notification, we will notify affected individuals and the Personal Data Protection Commission as required.

10. Changes to this notice

This privacy notice may be amended or updated from time to time. We will post the revised notice on this page and update the "Last updated" date. Your continued use of the waitlist after an update constitutes an acknowledgement of the revised notice.
